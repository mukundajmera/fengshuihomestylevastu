**Step 1: Obtain a Compass**
- Smartphone compass app (iPhone Compass, Android Compass 360)
- Traditional magnetic compass
- **Calibrate:** Move away from metal objects, electronics (10-foot distance)

**Step 2: Stand in Your Space's Center**
- If home: Stand in geometric center (see Brahmasthan guide for exact method)
- If office: Stand in center of your primary workspace (office, cubicle, desk area)

**Step 3: Identify Cardinal Directions**

**EAST:**
- Face the direction where the sun rises
- Compass reads **67.5° to 112.5°**

**SOUTH-EAST:**
- Stand in center, turn 45° clockwise from East
- Compass reads **112.5° to 157.5°**
