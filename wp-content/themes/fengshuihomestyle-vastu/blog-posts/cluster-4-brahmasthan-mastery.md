## Part 3: The 9-Grid Map (How to Locate Your Brahmasthan Precisely)

### The Vastu Mandala System

Vastu divides any floor plan into a **9x9 grid** (81 squares total). Each square governs a specific function. The **central 9 squares** (out of 81) form the Brahmasthan.

**Here's how to find yours:**

#### **Step 1: Obtain Your Floor Plan**

- If you have architectural drawings, use those
- If not, sketch your home's perimeter (doesn't need to be perfect)
- Include all load-bearing walls, but ignore furniture

#### **Step 2: Mark the 4 Corner Points**

- Stand at the extreme **North-East** corner → Mark point A
- Stand at the extreme **South-East** corner → Mark point B
- Stand at the extreme **South-West** corner → Mark point C
- Stand at the extreme **North-West** corner → Mark point D

**For irregular shapes:** Use the outermost points in each direction

#### **Step 3: Draw Crossing Diagonals**

- Draw a line from **A (NE) to C (SW)**
- Draw a line from **B (SE) to D (NW)**
- **Where they intersect = Your home's exact center point**

#### **Step 4: Map the Brahmasthan Zone**

**Option A (Simple Approximation):**
- Measure your home's longest wall (let's say 30 feet)
- Calculate 11% of that length (30 × 0.11 = 3.3 feet)
- Draw a square centered on the intersection point, with sides = 3.3 feet
- **That square is your Brahmasthan**

**Option B (Precise 9-Grid Method):**
- Divide your floor plan into 9 equal sections (like a tic-tac-toe grid)
- The **center square** of that grid = Brahmasthan

**Visual Example:**

│  SW  │  S   │  SE  │
